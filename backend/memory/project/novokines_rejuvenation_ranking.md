# Novokines for Rejuvenation: Design and Ranking

## Executive Summary

Based on the concept of de novo designed synthetic ligands that induce proximity between novel receptor subunit pairs (termed "Novokines"), we have designed and ranked 8 candidate Novokines for rejuvenation applications. The top candidates target key aging pathways including sirtuin activation, mTOR inhibition, and metabolic reprogramming.

## Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Biological Plausibility | 25% | Evidence linking receptors to aging/rejuvenation pathways |
| Novelty of Pairing | 20% | How novel is the receptor pair combination |
| Safety Profile | 20% | Potential risks of activating/inhibiting these pathways |
| Design Feasibility | 15% | Technical feasibility of designing the synthetic ligand |
| Therapeutic Potential | 20% | Potential impact on multiple hallmarks of aging |

## Ranked Novokine Candidates

### #1: S1R3 (Score: 7.95/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: SIRT1 activator domain + IGF1R inhibitory domain
- **Mechanism**: Simultaneously activate SIRT1 (longevity pathway) and inhibit IGF1R (pro-aging pathway)
- **Aging Relevance**: SIRT1 declines with age, IGF1R signaling promotes aging
- **Expected Effect**: Mimic caloric restriction, improve mitochondrial function, enhance stress resistance
- **Key Advantages**: Targets two major aging pathways simultaneously, strong literature support

### #2: mTORAMPK (Score: 7.8/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: mTOR inhibitory domain + AMPK activating domain
- **Mechanism**: Simultaneously inhibit mTOR (pro-aging) and activate AMPK (pro-longevity)
- **Aging Relevance**: mTOR hyperactivated in aging, AMPK declines with age
- **Expected Effect**: Mimic rapamycin + metformin effects, promote autophagy, improve metabolism
- **Key Advantages**: Combines two most validated longevity interventions

### #3: H2F (Score: 7.45/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: HER2 (ERBB2) + FGFR
- **Mechanism**: Induces proximity between HER2 (no natural ligand) and FGFR
- **Aging Relevance**: HER2 overexpression in aging, FGFR involved in tissue repair
- **Expected Effect**: Activate tissue repair pathways while modulating growth signals
- **Key Advantages**: Based on published concept, novel pairing with no natural ligand

### #4: TNFPPAR (Score: 7.3/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: TNF-R blocking domain + PPAR-γ activating domain
- **Mechanism**: Reduce inflammation while improving metabolic function
- **Aging Relevance**: TNF-α increases with age, PPAR-γ declines affecting metabolism
- **Expected Effect**: Anti-inflammatory and metabolic improvement
- **Key Advantages**: Targets inflammaging and metabolic dysfunction simultaneously

### #5: IL6TGF (Score: 7.25/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: IL-6R blocking domain + TGF-βR activating domain
- **Mechanism**: Block inflammaging (IL-6) while promoting tissue repair (TGF-β)
- **Aging Relevance**: IL-6 drives inflammaging, TGF-β promotes fibrosis in aging
- **Expected Effect**: Reduce chronic inflammation while modulating tissue repair
- **Key Advantages**: Addresses both inflammation and tissue repair deficits

### #6: PD1CXCR4 (Score: 7.05/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: PD-1 blocking domain + CXCR4 activating domain
- **Mechanism**: Reverse T-cell exhaustion while promoting stem cell homing
- **Aging Relevance**: PD-1 upregulation in immunosenescence, CXCR4 for stem cell migration
- **Expected Effect**: Rejuvenate immune cells and promote tissue regeneration
- **Key Advantages**: Targets immunosenescence and stem cell mobilization

### #7: VEGFRFGF (Score: 6.65/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: VEGFR2 + FGFR1
- **Mechanism**: Coordinate angiogenesis and tissue growth for regeneration
- **Aging Relevance**: Impaired angiogenesis in aging, reduced tissue repair
- **Expected Effect**: Enhance vascularization and tissue regeneration
- **Key Advantages**: Promotes tissue regeneration through vascular support

### #8: EGFRNOTCH (Score: 6.65/10)
- **Receptor Pair**: EGFR inhibitory domain + Notch activating domain
- **Mechanism**: Reduce hyperproliferative signals while promoting differentiation
- **Aging Relevance**: EGFR often hyperactivated in aging tissues, Notch regulates stemness
- **Expected Effect**: Balance proliferation and differentiation for tissue homeostasis
- **Key Advantages**: Maintains tissue homeostasis and stem cell function

## Design Considerations

1. **Delivery Systems**: Consider cell-penetrating peptides, nanoparticles, or viral vectors
2. **Tissue Specificity**: Add targeting domains for specific tissues (brain, muscle, immune cells)
3. **Tunable Activity**: Design with allosteric regulation or protease-cleavable linkers
4. **Combination Strategies**: Consider sequential or combination therapy with existing approaches
5. **Biomarker Development**: Establish predictive biomarkers for patient stratification
6. **Manufacturing**: Consider scalability and stability of synthetic ligand production

## Risk Assessment

### High Priority Risks:
1. **Oncogenic potential**: Activation of growth pathways (HER2, EGFR, IGF1R)
2. **Immune dysregulation**: Over-activation or suppression of immune responses
3. **Metabolic disruption**: Unintended effects on glucose/lipid metabolism

### Mitigation Strategies:
1. **Dose optimization**: Titrate to therapeutic window
2. **Tissue targeting**: Limit exposure to sensitive tissues
3. **Monitoring**: Regular assessment of safety biomarkers
